## English

### What the licence covers and what it does not

The source code is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (`LICENSE`). That licence grants rights to the **code**. It does
**not** grant any right to use:

- the names **G醫t**, **G-I-T** and **giiit** (the product, package and binary name),
- the app icon and logo (`icon.png`, `src-tauri/icons/`),
- the address of the dashboard as if it were yours (`giiit.goudge.org`).

### What you may do without asking

- Build the app for your own use, and modify it for your own use.
- Describe the project truthfully: "reads the feeds of the G醫t dashboard", "compatible with G醫t", a link to this repository, a
  bug report, a review, an article.
- Redistribute an **unmodified** official build under its own name, with the licence and this file left in.
- Use the name inside the app's own text where it only says what data the app reads (the dashboard is called G醫t).

### What you may not do without written permission

- Distribute a **modified** version or a **fork** under the names G醫t, G-I-T or giiit, or with the icon. Choose another name and
  another icon.
- Use a name or icon that could be taken for the official one (for example "G醫t Pro", "giiit Plus", "G醫t (official)", a look-alike
  icon, a typo-squatted store listing or domain).
- Say or suggest that a build, a service or a store listing is made, approved or endorsed by the owner when it is not.
- Remove or fake the build and version line under 設定 → このアプリについて, or present a modified build as the official one.
- Publish an app in any store (Microsoft Store, App Store, Google Play, …) under these names or icons. The store listings with these
  names belong to the owner.

### Modified and unofficial builds

The paid plan, the trial and the license keys are part of the code and can be changed by anyone who builds the app. This policy does
not try to stop that (see AGENTS.md §5.5); it only says that such a build is **not** the official app and must not be called by its
name. Support is given for official builds only.
